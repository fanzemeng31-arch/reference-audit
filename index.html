Reference Audit

Paste your reference list. Each entry is looked up in Crossref and OpenAlex, the two largest open indexes of scholarly work. You see which references exist, which carry a wrong year, author or DOI, and which have been retracted.

What the labels mean

Found
A record with this title exists, and its first author and year agree with your reference.
Check
A record exists, but something differs: the year, the first author, the DOI, or part of the title. Invented references often look like this, and so do honest typing errors.
Retracted
The work exists and has been retracted. Cite it only if you discuss the retraction.
No record
Neither index holds a matching record. Both indexes cover most journal articles and miss many books, reports, theses and works not written in English. Check these by hand.

What happens to your text

This page has no server of its own. Your browser sends each reference straight to Crossref and OpenAlex, which are public, non-profit indexes, and shows you what they return. Nothing you paste is stored or seen by this site. Paste the reference list only; the rest of your manuscript is not needed.

Limits

The check tells you whether a work exists and whether its details agree with yours. It does not read the work, so it cannot tell you whether the work supports the sentence you cite it for. A label of “Found” means the reference is real, and the reading is still yours to do.

Other ways to use it

The same check runs from the terminal, and as a skill for AI coding agents such as Claude Code. The script reads a text file or a BibTeX file and prints a report. The instructions are on GitHub.
